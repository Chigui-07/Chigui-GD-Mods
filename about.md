# Demon Tracker

**Demon Tracker** builds a personal hardest-to-easiest ranking from the demons you actually complete in Geometry Dash.

## How it works

When you complete a new demon, Demon Tracker asks six short questions about how difficult the level felt **to you at the time you completed it**:

- General difficulty
- Consistency
- Precision
- Control
- Learning
- Pressure

The answers are combined into a personal Difficulty Score and the level is inserted automatically into your ranking.

Once you confirm the questionnaire, that level's relative placement against demons already in your list is permanent. Future completions can still be inserted above, below, or between existing entries.

## Features

- Global personal demon ranking.
- Separate Easy, Medium, Hard, Insane, and Extreme Demon lists.
- Automatic questionnaire after a valid new completion.
- Persistent local save data.
- Rebeats keep the existing classification and update rebeat statistics.
- Level creator, ID, attempts, personal score, and rank information.
- Tap a tracked level to open its detail page and jump back to the level in Geometry Dash.
- Native Geometry Dash difficulty faces and UI styling.

## Completion rule

A new demon is classified after reaching **100%**. If the level has verified coins, all of those coins must also be collected before Demon Tracker will classify it.

## Privacy

Demon Tracker stores its ranking locally through Geode's saved-data system. It does not upload your personal ranking to an external service.

## Platforms

Initial public support targets **Windows** and **Android**.
